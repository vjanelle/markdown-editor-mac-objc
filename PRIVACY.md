# Privacy Policy for Draftmark

Effective date: July 20, 2026

Draftmark does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Documents and preferences remain on the user's Mac. The app accesses files only when the user chooses to open or save them. Markdown preview is rendered locally.

The app does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or remote servers. External links open in the user's default browser only when explicitly clicked.

For support, visit the [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/vjanelle/Draftmark/issues).

We may update this policy if the app's data practices change. Any updated policy will be published at this URL.
